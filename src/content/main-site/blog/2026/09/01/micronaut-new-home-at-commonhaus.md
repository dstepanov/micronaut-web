Today, we are excited to share that Micronaut’s move to the [Commonhaus Foundation](https://www.commonhaus.org/) is almost complete. We are finalizing some assets transfers but **Micronaut is a project of the Commonhaus Foundation. .**

In January, we [announced our plans to join the Commonhaus Foundation](/2026/01/12/micronaut-announces-plans-to-join-the-commonhaus-foundation/). This move marks an important new chapter for Micronaut and provides a long-term home for the project and the people who build, use, and support it.

## A durable home for Micronaut

Commonhaus is a non-profit foundation focused on the sustainability of open source libraries and frameworks. Its approach aligns closely with the values that have guided Micronaut from the beginning: respect for project identity, support without unnecessary mandates, transparency, and long-term thinking.

For the Micronaut community, this provides a neutral and durable home for the project. Commonhaus can help with the legal, administrative, and organizational work that supports a healthy open source ecosystem, while the Micronaut community and maintainers continue to guide the framework’s technical direction and day-to-day development.

The move also creates more opportunities for individuals and organizations to participate in and support Micronaut. You can learn more about the foundation’s model and community on the [Commonhaus website](https://www.commonhaus.org/), where Micronaut is now listed among its projects.

## What remains the same

Micronaut remains open source and available under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). The project’s repositories, issue trackers, and contribution workflows remain available through the [Micronaut GitHub organization](https://github.com/micronaut-projects).

Most importantly, the people and principles at the heart of Micronaut remain the same. The framework will continue to focus on productive, modular, easily testable applications with fast startup and a small footprint across the JVM and cloud-native environments.

## Join us for what comes next

We invite individuals and organizations to participate in the Micronaut project, contribute to its future, and help strengthen the broader open source ecosystem. Visit the [Commonhaus website](https://www.commonhaus.org/) to learn more about Micronaut’s new home.

Thank you to everyone who has contributed code, documentation, reviews, ideas, sponsorship, and encouragement over the years. We are grateful to the Micronaut community, to the Micronaut and Unity Foundations, and to the Commonhaus Foundation for helping us reach this next stage.

We are excited to continue building Micronaut with you in its new home.
